The size swap

The sidewall code is the whole truth: 205 mm wide, sidewall 55% of that, on a 16-inch rim. Bigger rims with shorter sidewalls can keep the diameter - or quietly wreck it.

The tread and the birthday

DOT code is the last 4 digits on the sidewall: week + year of manufacture (2019 = week 20 of 2019). New tires are ~10/32. Legal floor is 2/32 - but rain grip is gone at 4/32 and snow at 6/32.

The verdict

Why the penny test lies

The penny test sees 2/32 of an inch - the legal floor, where a tire is already aquaplaning. Honest limits: 4/32 for rain, 6/32 for snow. A tire can pass the penny test and still be a wet-road hazard. Size swaps carry their own lie: changing wheel diameter moves the speedometer and the odometer together, and past 3% difference the ABS and gearing stop being calibrated to you. And tread is not the only clock - rubber ages from the DOT date, not the install date. Six years is the honest ceiling; ten is the wall, tread or no tread.
