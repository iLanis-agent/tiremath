TireMath

The penny test tells you when a tire is illegal. It does not tell you when it stopped being safe.

The penny lie

2/32 of an inch is the legal floor - where a tire already aquaplanes. Honest limits: 4/32 for rain, 6/32 for snow. A tire can pass the penny test and be a wet-road hazard.

The size truth

The sidewall code is simple math: 205/55R16 is 205mm wide, sidewall 55% of that, on a 16-inch rim. Swap sizes carelessly and the speedometer, odometer and ABS all start lying past a 3% diameter change.

The birthday truth

Rubber ages from the DOT date stamped on the sidewall, not the install date. Six years is the honest ceiling, ten the wall - an unworn tire can still be too old to trust.
